Privacy Policy

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you visit Facades Tell Stories.

1. Who we are

Facades Tell Stories (“we”, “us”, “our”) is an independent editorial journal covering interior design, architecture, materials, and the built environment. Our website is facadestellstories.com.

For the purposes of the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), we act as the data controller in respect of any personal data we collect through this website.

2. Data we collect

We may collect and process the following categories of personal data:

  • Information you provide voluntarily. When you use our contact form, subscribe to a newsletter, or email us directly, you may provide your name, email address, and the content of your message.
  • Automatically collected information. Like most websites, we may automatically collect certain technical data when you visit — including your IP address, browser type and version, time zone setting, operating system, and pages you view. This is used for analytics and to improve the site.
  • Cookies and similar technologies. We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to understand how you interact with the site. You can control cookies through your browser settings. See our Cookies Policy for full details.
  • Search queries. When you use our site search, we may log the search terms to improve our content and search functionality. These logs are not linked to your identity.

3. How we use your data

We use the personal data we collect for the following purposes:

  • To respond to your enquiries, pitches, and correspondence.
  • To send you editorial updates or newsletters, if you have opted in (you can unsubscribe at any time).
  • To analyse site usage and improve the performance, content, and user experience of Facades Tell Stories.
  • To maintain the security and integrity of our website and prevent fraudulent or abusive activity.
  • To comply with legal obligations to which we are subject.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Legal basis for processing

Under UK and EU data protection law, we must have a lawful basis for processing your personal data. We rely on the following:

  • Consent — where you have opted in to receive communications or accepted non-essential cookies.
  • Legitimate interests — to respond to your enquiries, improve our website, and ensure its security, in a way that does not override your rights.
  • Contract — where processing is necessary to fulfil a contract with you (for example, commissioning editorial work).
  • Legal obligation — where we are required to process data to comply with the law.

5. Cookies & tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies to enhance your browsing experience, analyse site traffic, and remember your preferences. Cookies are small text files stored on your device.

You can set your browser to refuse all or some cookies, or to alert you when websites set or access cookies. If you disable or refuse cookies, please note that some parts of this website may become inaccessible or not function properly.

For detailed information about the cookies we use and how to manage them, please see our Cookies Policy.

6. Third-party services

We may use trusted third-party service providers to help us operate the website and deliver our services. These may include:

  • Analytics providers (e.g. privacy-focused analytics) to understand how visitors use the site.
  • Email service providers to manage newsletter subscriptions and correspondence.
  • Hosting providers to store and serve the website.

These providers are contractually obligated to protect your data and may only process it in accordance with our instructions. Some providers may be located outside the UK/EEA; where this is the case, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place (such as Standard Contractual Clauses).

7. Data retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, including any legal, accounting, or reporting requirements.

  • Contact form submissions and email correspondence are typically retained for up to 24 months, unless a longer period is required for legal reasons.
  • Newsletter subscription data is retained until you unsubscribe or request deletion.
  • Analytics data is retained in an aggregated or anonymised form and is not linked to individuals.

When data is no longer needed, we securely delete or anonymise it.

8. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of access — to request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to rectification — to ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Right to erasure — to request that we delete your personal data, in certain circumstances.
  • Right to restrict processing — to ask us to limit how we use your data.
  • Right to data portability — to receive your data in a structured, commonly used format.
  • Right to object — to object to processing based on legitimate interests or for direct marketing.
  • Right to withdraw consent — where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details in section 12. We will respond within one month, as required by law. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority (in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office).

9. Security

We take the security of your personal data seriously. We have implemented appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction of your information.

These measures include encrypted connections (HTTPS), access controls, and regular reviews of our data practices. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Children's privacy

Our website is not directed at children under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a child, please contact us so we can take appropriate steps to delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised “last updated” date.

We encourage you to review this policy periodically to stay informed about how we protect your information.

12. Contact us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, or wish to exercise your data protection rights, please contact us:

Email: privacy@facadestellstories.com
General enquiries: hello@facadestellstories.com

We aim to respond to all privacy-related enquiries within 5 working days.

This Privacy Policy should be read alongside our Terms of Use and Cookies Policy.
