Pitch guidelines

We publish in-depth essays, practical guides, and critical perspectives on interior design, architecture, materials, and the stories buildings tell. Before you pitch, please consider:

  • We favour pieces with a clear point of view, grounded in real projects or material research.
  • Feature essays run 1,200–2,500 words; guides and case studies 800–1,500 words.
  • Include 2–3 proposed images or a moodboard direction with your pitch, where possible.
  • We pay for accepted work. Rates are discussed on commissioning.
  • Simultaneous pitches are fine, but let us know if it's been placed elsewhere.
